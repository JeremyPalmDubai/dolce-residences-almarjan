A few things
to know.

Where is Dolce Residences by Wyndham?

The supplied brochure locates Dolce on plot HRA08, Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates.

What is the starting price?

The communicated project starting price is AED 1,400,000, approximately USD 381,212. Individual prices and availability must be confirmed; no price is assigned here to a particular typology.

What is the payment plan?

30% on reservation and 70% at handover. At AED 1,400,000, that is AED 420,000 followed by AED 980,000, excluding fees and other costs.

When is handover expected?

The concept brochure indicates Q4 2028 as a tentative completion target. This is not a guaranteed contractual delivery date.

Are the interior images final specifications?

No. The brochure labels them as interior moods courtesy Wyndham. Project concept renders and destination illustrations are identified separately.

Does a purchase qualify for a Golden Visa?

Eligibility is conditional, requires authority approval and depends on the applicable rules and supporting documents. The ICP real-estate investor route states a minimum property value of AED 2,000,000. A purchase of AED 1,400,000 alone is below that threshold.
